About

Built by strategists, not salespeople.

Tax Artists started with a simple frustration: too many firms treat tax season as a once-a-year scramble. We built a practice around the opposite idea — steady, year-round partnership.

How we started

A small practice, on purpose.

We kept the team small so every client works directly with the person who actually knows their file — not a rotating cast of preparers during crunch season.

That means fewer clients per strategist, more time spent planning instead of just filing, and a habit of picking up the phone before a small issue becomes an expensive one.

It's slower to scale. We're fine with that.

The team

Who you'll actually talk to.

Larry Steinberg
Founder & Chief Investment Officer

Larry Steinberg is an experienced financial adviser, speaker, and author. He combines the endowment model of investing with tax strategy to create financial peace of mind. Larry started as an advisor at age 23, started his own shop at 25, and currently has been a licensed advisor for over 30 years.

As a partner and the Chief Investment Officer of Financial Architects and Cabin Advisors, he guides the firms' investment management using alternative and traditional investments focusing on after tax returns and spendable income for families, trusts, retirement plans, donor advised funds and captive insurance.

Gifted with a booming voice, Larry speaks on taxes, alternative investments, indexing, and entrepreneurship at various conferences and events around the country. He also has used that voice closer to home to coach his kid's in their sports. Larry is a big believer in athletics for both skill and character development.

Please note that there are special rules & risks associated with alternative investments & not every investor will be eligible or suitable to invest in alternative investments. Many alternative investments place limitations on who may purchase them based a person's income & assets & some may only be available to accredited investors. Alternative Investments often include a high degree of risk.

Martin Suarez
IRS Enrolled Agent & Life Insurance Professional

Martin Suarez is a dual-licensed IRS Enrolled Agent and life insurance professional who helps clients navigate complex tax, insurance, and retirement decisions with clarity and confidence.

He works proactively with high-income individuals and business owners to reduce lifetime tax exposure, improve cash flow, and align financial decisions with long-term wealth and legacy goals — always within the letter and spirit of the law.

Martin is known for a disciplined, suitability-first approach to permanent life insurance and annuities, designing strategies that prioritize tax efficiency, income reliability, principal protection, and long-term flexibility. His work integrates tax planning and retirement income into a single, cohesive strategy — so clients receive guidance they can trust, not disconnected products.
